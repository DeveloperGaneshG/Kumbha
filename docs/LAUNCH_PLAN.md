# Launch plan — first 3 weeks

No website becomes popular on its own; people have to see it and share it. This plan uses what Kumbha already has — the free greeting card maker, WhatsApp ordering, combos and the Diwali deadline — to get the first customers talking.

## Before launch (2–3 days)

1. **Real photos.** Replace the Unsplash stand-ins with your own: each product on a plain background, plus one photo of it in use. Phone photos in daylight are fine. Real photos sell handmade goods far better than stock.
2. **Real numbers and reviews.** Replace the sample stats and testimonials in `src/config/store.ts`. Ask 5–10 past customers for a one-line quote and a photo.
3. **Go live.** Deploy to Vercel (free), connect your domain, set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL` and `NEXT_PUBLIC_WHATSAPP_NUMBER`.
4. **Google Business Profile.** Create or update it for the Vijayawada store with photos, hours and the website link. This is how local "clay pots near me" and "bricks near me" searches find you.
5. **WhatsApp Business.** Set up the catalogue, a greeting message, quick replies for prices and delivery, and labels for new / paid / delivered orders.

## Week 1 — greeting cards

- Make 5 greeting cards yourself and post them as WhatsApp status and Instagram stories with the link "make yours free".
- Send the greeting card link to every customer and family group you have: free, useful, no hard sell. Every card shared carries the Kumbha name.
- Post one short reel a day: a potter at the wheel, painting diyas, an Etikoppaka toy being turned, a tractor unloading bricks. Process videos of crafts are some of the most shared content in this space.

## Week 2 — Diwali deadline

- Broadcast the **Diwali Puja Combo** and the **"order by 3 Nov"** deadline on WhatsApp.
- Offer a small thank-you (free diya set) for customers who post their order with **#MyKumbhaHome**, then feature their photos in the "Styled at home" section.
- Contact 10 apartment associations and offices for **corporate and return-gift** orders using the Festive Gift Box.

## Week 3 — builders

- Visit 20 local contractors and hardware shops with a printed card (logo files are in `public/brand/`) pointing to the brick calculator and "Share your requirement".
- Offer credit terms to the first few regular contractors.

## Measure

Add Google Analytics or Vercel Analytics and watch three numbers each week: visitors, WhatsApp chats started, and orders. Double down on whatever brings the most chats.
